01
Horizon O — Surface Protection

Zero Tillage & Structural Soil Integrity

Mechanical plowing shreds delicate fungal hyphae and oxidizes stored organic carbon. Eliminating tillage preserves intact macro-aggregates, allowing glomalin protein matrices to shield the earth's natural architecture.

02
Horizon A — Topsoil Stratum

Multi-Species Cover Crop Armor

Bare soil is wounded soil. Polyculture cover mixes (crimson clover, daikon radish, hairy vetch) provide living photosynthesis year-round, regulating root temperatures by up to 25°F and arresting erosion.

03
Horizon B — Rhizosphere Matrix

Liquid Carbon & Mycorrhizal Commerce

Plant roots pump up to 40% of their photosynthetic sugar exudates deep into the earth. In return, mycorrhizal fungi mine rock phosphorus, zinc, and micronutrients over 100 meters away to nourish the plant.

04
Horizon C — Deep Subsoil & Bedrock

Deep Taproots & Permanent Carbon Sinks

Deep-penetrating taproots break hardpan layers and channel carbon into stable humic complexes. This permanent subterranean sink locks atmospheric CO2 into biological reserves for decades.
