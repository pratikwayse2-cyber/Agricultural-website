Hydraulic Engineering

Technical Specifications

Comprehensive engineering details for irrigation planners, agronomists, and soil scientists.

The Physics of Differential Pressure: In standard gravity-fed tubing, hydrostatic pressure decreases as elevation rises or as distance increases from the pump manifold. AgriBot PC emitters utilize an engineered medical-grade silicone diaphragm suspended over an elastomeric labyrinth channel. As inlet pressure fluctuates between 10 PSI and 60 PSI, the diaphragm flexes dynamically, regulating the discharge aperture to maintain an exact, fixed flow rate (±1.5%) across every single plant in the field.
Anti-Siphon (AS) & Root Guard Technology: When the irrigation cycle terminates, standard drip lines create a temporary vacuum that draws surrounding dirt and fine root hairs into the nozzle. AgriBot emitters feature an integrated mechanical anti-siphon check-valve that instantly seals shut upon depressurization. Additionally, our emitter resin is infused with copper oxide micro-particles that harmlessly deflect root hairs without releasing harmful chemicals into the edible crop harvest.
Closed-Loop Agronomic Automation: Dual-depth FDR (Frequency Domain Reflectometry) soil probes continuously read volumetric water content (VWC) and electrical conductivity (EC). When soil nutrient osmolarity drops below calibrated crop thresholds, the central fertigation injector dissolves water-soluble nitrogen, potassium, and phosphorus directly into the irrigation stream at precise parts-per-million (PPM) concentrations.
